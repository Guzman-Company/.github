### About us

Guzman & Company is an investment bank and brokerage firm providing capital
markets and trading services to corporate, institutional, and high-net-worth
clients.

[guzman.com →](https://www.guzman.com)

[FINRA BrokerCheck →](https://brokercheck.finra.org/firm/summary/21013)

### In-house operations

Unlike many firms our size, Guzman & Company runs its own operations
department. Our in-house team handles trade processing, matching, and
settlement directly, supported by an integrated back-office platform —
so clients get fast, consistent resolution without handoffs.

[Learn more →](https://www.guzman.com/about-guzman/in-house-operations/)

### Engineering at Guzman

Our technology team builds the internal platforms that support trading,
reporting, and operations across the firm. We focus on reliability, data
accuracy, and modernizing our systems with cloud-native tooling.
